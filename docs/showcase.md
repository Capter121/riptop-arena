# RIPTOP Arena Showcase

## Pitch

RIPTOP Arena is a fast, single-player 3D battle-top prototype built for browser play. It takes the drama of high-speed arena collisions and turns it into a compact, replayable loop: launch, clash, adapt your build, and climb a short tournament ladder for new parts and champion crowns.

This is not a systems-heavy sandbox or a giant service game. It is a focused action slice built to answer one question well:

**Can a browser-based battle-top game feel sharp, readable, and exciting in under a minute?**

## Why It Works

The prototype leans on three strengths:

### 1. Fast readability

Rounds start quickly, impacts are easy to parse, and finish states are clear. You can understand what is happening even when the tops are moving fast because the camera, HUD, flashes, and finish calls all reinforce the same moment.

### 2. Visible build identity

The part system is intentionally compact. Each combination changes the feel of the top in a noticeable way, so players can immediately understand the difference between aggression, stability, mobility, and endurance.

### 3. Reward rhythm

The tournament ladder adds momentum beyond a single fight. Wins unlock new parts, rare gear is surfaced clearly, and full clears are recorded on a champion shelf so the run has a real endpoint.

## Core Loop

1. Choose a build in the garage.
2. Charge and angle the launch.
3. Dash for pressure, ring-outs, or burst damage.
4. Win a quick match or progress through the tournament ladder.
5. Unlock new parts and push for a full champion clear.

## Feature Highlights

- Cinematic intro sequence and layered menu presentation
- Dynamic battle camera with launch framing, close chase, and danger-edge emphasis
- Three victory routes: `ring out`, `spin finish`, `burst finish`
- Garage progression with locked parts, rarities, and collectible presentation
- Tournament ladder with unlock rewards and champion tracking
- Audio feedback for launches, clashes, bursts, ambience, and champion moments
- Mobile-friendly landscape controls without removing desktop input

## What Makes This Slice Showcase-Ready

This prototype is no longer just a rendering test or mechanics sandbox. It has the pieces people expect when they try a small action game for the first time:

- a strong first screen
- a complete playable round
- progression between rounds
- payoff for mastery
- clear audiovisual identity

That makes it suitable for:

- portfolio presentation
- prototype pitch demos
- small closed playtests
- static-host web deployment

## If You Hand This To A Player

Tell them three things:

1. Start with `Quick Battle` to feel the combat loop.
2. Move to `Tournament Run` for progression and unlocks.
3. Try different parts early because the build differences are meant to be felt, not hidden.
